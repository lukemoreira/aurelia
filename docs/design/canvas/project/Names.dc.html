The Ledger Thief

Will? had the ledger under his coat before Tomas could stop him. They ran the length of the Lantern Quarter, past the chandler’s door, and did not stop until the bridge.

“Will? you give it back?” Elena asked, and the Greywater bells answered for him.

Elena’s hand still hurt. She held it against her chest and counted the bells the way her mother had taught her.
