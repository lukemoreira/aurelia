# Aurelia

*Write worlds. Shape stories.*

Aurelia is a free, open-source, local-first studio for writing novels, history and
interactive (branching) fiction. Your manuscript, characters, places, lore and
research live together, linked — and they live in plain files on your own disk.

> **Status:** planning and design. Nothing to install yet. See [`PLANS.md`](PLANS.md).

## What it will do

- **Write** in a calm, focused editor with Markdown shortcuts and a toolbar.
- **Plan** with parts, chapters and scenes, arcs, a corkboard and an outline.
- **Branch** your story: give a scene choices, see the story map, play it through.
- **Build a world** that several books share: characters, places, items, events,
  lore, research and timelines.
- **Link everything** with `[[links]]` that never break when you rename things.
- **Own your work**: Markdown files in a folder you choose. Works offline. No account.
- Optional later: sync to your own cloud, and AI reviews you control.
